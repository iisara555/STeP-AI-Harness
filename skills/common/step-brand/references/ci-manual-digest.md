## Versions and backgrounds (page 4)

The examples show a full lockup, a shorter STeP/CMU wordmark, and a standalone symbol in each treatment:

- **Original Colour:** dark artwork with the yellow accent on white.
- **Mono - Colour / Light Background:** dark monochrome artwork on a light background (yellow is the example).
- **Mono - Colour / Dark Background:** white monochrome artwork on a dark background.
- **Photographic background:** the mark may be placed on a photograph when the image does not interfere with it and it remains clearly visible; select the supplied variant suited to the background.

Use matching official files for these variants. A screenshot from the digest is evidence, not a production logo asset. The yellow background example means a blanket ban on yellow backgrounds is not a CI rule.

## Minimum size and handling (page 5)

- The illustrated **full lockup** and **standalone symbol** each have a minimum **height of 8 mm**, measured across the complete illustrated mark. Page 5 does not specify a separate minimum for the short wordmark.
- Check physical size at the final printed output scale. The digest gives no pixel minimum for screen use; assess legibility at actual display size and request a digital specification when needed.
- Use original supplied logo files, preserve proportions, and choose format and resolution for the intended medium.
- Do not stretch/condense, redraw, create a variation, enclose the logo inside another graphic, add graphics to make a new mark, or use the logo as running text.
- Do not substitute a web download for a print-ready asset or shrink a mark below its specified minimum.
