# STeP TOR letterhead

The owner supplied STeP purchase and service TOR PDFs as the visual reference on 2026-10-27 and requested a STeP working template.

The small public organization wordmark in `desktop/electron/step-tor-logo.ts` was extracted, without resampling, from the embedded `Image36.png` on the first page of the supplied office-materials-management service TOR (`68_04`, four pages). It is 226 x 60 pixels, RGBA. PNG SHA-256: `57b33da19b017832ea93e405bd364645ca6acc49528496fded4a7607edad28fd`.

Only the organization mark is retained. Source PDFs, private project text, staff identities, signatures, prices and contractual conditions are excluded from the repository. The mark is used for STeP's own document tool and is not a general license for third-party use.

The working layout follows the examples' repeated letterhead, centered subject, restrained heading sizes, hanging numeric clauses and quiet footer. It does not certify a current procurement form, authorize signing, or import the examples' commercial or legal conditions.
